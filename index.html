    The person behind the builds.

    I'm a Unity developer based in Gurugram. I started with 2D platformers in 2020 and have since worked on mobile games, a hub app that hosts over a hundred of them, XR fitness and training apps, and multiplayer on Photon.

    Most of what I do sits where the game meets everything around it: editor tools that remove busywork, WebGL builds that load quickly, pipelines that ship without hand-holding. Lately I've been pushing further into the server side, the APIs, auth and services a live game depends on.
